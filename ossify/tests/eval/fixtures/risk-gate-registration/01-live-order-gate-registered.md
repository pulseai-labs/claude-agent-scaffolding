expected_register: yes
expected_controls: paper env,human confirm,kill switch,audit trail,progressive exposure

A trading skeleton's Release 0 places live orders against a broker: charging a real card, spending API credits at scale, and moving money that is gone once moved. The defect character is the money family — a test failure cannot undo a filled order. The proposed risk gate registers the touch surface `src/exec/**,src/broker/**` and the controls: a paper/sandbox environment as the default target with real mode opt-in and explicit; a human confirm that names the concrete effect ("sell 200 shares of X at market"), not a generic "are you sure?"; a kill switch flag that halts the order class without a redeploy; an append-only audit trail of who/what/when/inputs/outcome; and progressive exposure shipping to one symbol first and widening on evidence. The controls are the ones the menu attaches to the money family, not a uniform set applied to every gate. The skeleton reaches this surface in Release 0, so the gate is registered now. A spine whose plan touches `src/broker/**` reclassifies to bone and inherits this control checklist as required work, and the first release where the gate's surface becomes reachable is a docs trigger carrying threat/failure notes and an audit & recovery plan. A bone about the broker-adapter design also applies and is registered alongside the gate.
