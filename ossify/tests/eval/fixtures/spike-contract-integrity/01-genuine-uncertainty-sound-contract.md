A bone plans an agent-first modeling core that holds a coherent, editable 3D scene graph. Two candidate architectures differ so much that picking wrong means a rewrite — a persistent entity-array vs a live CRDT — and no cheap fact distinguishes them; the core product hypothesis itself is technical ("can an LLM-driven modeling loop produce a valid, editable scene graph from three successive natural-language edits?"). No cheaper sibling owns it: this is not a crate/version/API fact (smoke test), not a read-shaped comparison (research), and not an experiential "which shape" with no falsifier (prototype). The bone the spike enables is named: the modeling-core persistence bone.

The proposed spike's contract, written before any code: exactly one hypothesis — "an LLM-driven modeling loop can produce a valid, editable scene graph from three successive natural-language edits"; one falsifier written first — "any of the three edits corrupts the graph, or requires hand-editing the file to continue"; a wall-clock timebox of one day; `code_fate: discard` on a scratch branch `spike/scene-graph-crdt`, never merged, learned behavior to be reimplemented inside the product under normal spine ceremony; evidence retained — the edit transcripts and the graph snapshots; the decision it enables — the persistence bone. The hypothesis touches no risk gate and no live money, live customer data, or destructive surface.
